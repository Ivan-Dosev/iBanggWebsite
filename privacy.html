Privacy Policy

How we treat information in the app

Last updated: April 15, 2026

Plain summary: We do not collect personal information for our use, and we do not share data with third parties (see sections 1 and 2 below). How you physically handle your phone — and limits on our liability for damage or misuse — are covered in the Terms & Conditions (especially sections 2 and 4 there), not in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy describes how the iBangg mobile application (“the App”) treats information. The App is a casual, entertainment experience and is not intended to collect or monetize your data.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers through the App for our own use.

The App does not include active analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or social logins controlled by us for the purpose of tracking you across apps or websites.

2. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties because we do not collect it for those purposes. We do not use the App to build marketing profiles about you.

3. Local data on your device

Settings you choose in the App (for example, sound preferences) may be stored only on your device using standard on-device storage (such as iOS UserDefaults). The App also stores tokens and temporary sound unlock states locally on your device. All of this data stays on your phone unless your device backup or operating system copies it as part of your normal device backup—something we do not access or control.

4. Motion, camera, and system access

The App uses device motion to detect sharp movements and play sounds. The App may also request camera access solely to scan QR codes for the friend challenge feature. No images, video, or sensor data are stored or transmitted to us. All processing happens on your device.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional paid subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 in a way that requires parental consent frameworks. Parents and guardians should supervise device use by minors.

7. Changes

We may update this policy if the App changes materially. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about this App, contact the developer using the contact information provided in the App Store listing (if any).
